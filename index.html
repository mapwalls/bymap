Case 01 · Web · Dashboard · AI

AI Custom Reports

+42%users building reports

A module where HR admins build, manage and generate their own reports, then describe one in plain language and let AI set it up.

Read case study →
The problem
Clients had a long list of generic reports but couldn't combine values to create their own.
My decision
I reviewed Pendo data and interviewed HR admins first, then made the call to bring AI into report building and scoped an MVP-first release: a template or an AI-built report.
The result
2,842 users created a report (+42.3%) and building one became ~30% faster than manual configuration.

Case 02 · Mobile app · Payroll & HR

Benefits Enrollment

40→12%incomplete enrollments

The first fully mobile healthcare benefits enrollment for a payroll & HR platform: a dense desktop process turned into guided steps.

Read case study →
The problem
Enrollment only existed as a dense web flow. 52% of surveyed employees were missing the total cost per paycheck, and 72% delayed because of too many options or no way to compare plans.
My decision
One clear step at a time: per-paycheck cost on every plan and tier, side-by-side comparison, and a home card that surfaces progress during open enrollment.
The result
Incomplete or support-assisted enrollments dropped from ~40% to ~12% (product analytics and support data).

Case 03 · Responsive web · Payroll & HR

New Hires Onboarding

91%completion rate

A self-guided onboarding for employees and one review dashboard for HR, across desktop, tablet and mobile.

Read case study →
The problem
Onboarding was scattered across emails, PDFs and portals. New hires didn't know what was left, and HR spent much of its time chasing reminders.
My decision
After interviewing 11 employees, I designed a step-by-step wizard with save-and-resume and a progress tracker, plus an admin dashboard to approve or reject submissions.
The result
91% completion, onboarding time from 5 to 2 days, 40% faster admin approvals and 70% fewer support requests.

Case 04 · Design system · Payroll

GS Design System

70+components

A single source of truth for a payroll platform used by thousands of HR administrators.

Read case study →
The problem
The product had grown organically: buttons, forms and tables looked and behaved differently in every module.
My decision
Audit first, then build tokens, 70+ components and documentation in Figma with variables and auto layout, designed to WCAG 2.1 AA.
The result
Faster design-to-dev handoff, a consistent UI across 4+ product areas and one shared library for designers, developers and PMs.
