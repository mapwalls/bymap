Case Study 01

Lux Voyage Trip Hub

An operations hub for a luxury travel agency. Calls, emails and WhatsApp messages land in one place, an AI assistant drafts the record, and advisors only review, correct and approve.

Industry Luxury Travel
Client Lux Voyage, via Singular
Role Senior Product Designer
Platform Web App (internal)

The challenge

Lux Voyage plans high-touch trips where every detail comes from a conversation: a call with the client, an email from a supplier, a WhatsApp thread with a local partner. All of that knowledge had to end up in trips, tasks and client records, and most of it was being carried there by hand.

Problem

The work lived in three inboxes

Client conversations were spread across calls, email and WhatsApp. Advisors retyped what they heard into the trip, the tasks and the client profile, and covering a teammate on PTO meant digging through someone else's inbox to understand where a trip stood.

Solution

One place to review and approve

A trip hub where every conversation arrives on its own, an assistant reads it and proposes the record, and the advisor reviews, fixes what is missing and approves. The same hub shows the day's priorities, the team's trips and the tasks each trip needs.

Stage 01

Discovery

I worked with the agency's advisors and operations team to map how a conversation becomes a booked trip: who takes the call, where the notes go, which checklist applies to each stage and what happens when the person in charge is away.

1

Context was scattered. The same trip had pieces in a call recording, an email chain and a WhatsApp group, with no single place to see them together.

2

Typing was the bottleneck. Advisors spent their time moving information between tools instead of designing trips for their clients.

3

Coverage was fragile. When someone was on PTO, the team had no shared view of their conversations or trips.

4

Standards lived in people's heads. The SOP for each trip stage existed, but following it depended on memory.

Stage 02

The principle

Every flow in the product follows one rule: the assistant proposes, the person approves. The AI does the reading and the drafting; a human always decides what becomes part of the record. A few rules came out of that principle and shaped every screen:

A

Private stays private. Conversations marked private at the source never enter the platform; everything else is visible to the whole team.

B

Nothing is approved half-done. A trip conversation can only be approved once it is linked to a client and a trip.

C

Anyone can cover. Reviewers can approve on behalf of the person who took the call, and every trip shows its owner and collaborators.

Stage 03

Design solution

My Day: what needs me now

The home screen answers one question: what do I need to do today? Active trips, conversations waiting for review, clients currently travelling and the next departures, with tasks grouped by trip and split into Now and Later.

Conversations: one review queue

Calls, emails and WhatsApp threads arrive in a single grid with their type, status and source. The list starts on Pending Review, so the queue is the default view; approved and rejected conversations stay available, nothing is deleted.

Tasks: the SOP, built in

Each task has one owner and lives under its trip. Pre-departure, quality control and playbook tasks arrive ready from the SOP, so advisors only check them off, and assigning a task to someone sends them a notification.

Trips: every stage at a glance

My trips and Team trips share one list and Kanban view, with the stage, the trip manager and the task progress of every trip. Closed trips move to an archive grouped by Completed, Lost and Cancelled.

Stage 04

Iterations & outcome

The product grew through three prototype versions reviewed with the client, followed by a full visual restyle with a lighter palette, pastel status chips, record pages, notifications and empty states. Each change was written down as a user story with acceptance rules, so every flow is defined before it reaches development.

20% less time spent on each trip

AI agents collect what clients say in emails and phone calls and attach it to the right client and trip. Advisors now find everything about a client in seconds, in one trip record, instead of going back to call transcripts or manual notes.

The result: the team cut its working time by 20%, and that time goes back into designing trips.
